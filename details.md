**Project Overview: TRADE BUDDY**

**Purpose of the App**

- The app serves as a portal for students on our campus to trade items they no longer need.
- Students will be able to exchange or trade items throughout the campus.
- This platform helps students avoid unnecessary spending on items they will only use temporarily.

**Example:**

If a student needs a calculator during their four years of study, they can sell it for half the price on our site after they no longer need it. They can use the proceeds to trade for other items or collect money from other students who may have lost their calculators or are new to the campus.

**How the App Works:**

- Two students can connect through our portal to agree on the trade of one or more items and meet at a designated location on campus for the exchange.
- The app will be available 24/7.
- Meeting locations will adhere to campus rules as long as the campus is open.
- Transactions can be conducted either in person or through online payment options, with cash on delivery available if preferred.
- Students can communicate through our app or send messages to share details regarding the trade.
- The app uses end-to-end encryption to ensure users' details remain private.
- A search function allows users to find items based on categories and specific objects.
- Separate pages will be available for users wishing to trade items and for those looking to make monetary transactions.
- Trust points will be assigned to frequent users and traders on the app to enhance reliability.
